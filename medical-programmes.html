Medical student knowledge centre

Everything to check before applying

🩺 Academic Entry

Medical and dental admissions often require strong Biology, Chemistry and Physics/Mathematics results, and possibly an interview. Equivalency is assessed individually.

🗣️ English Requirements

IELTS, TOEFL and other accepted tests vary by university and professional programme. A general university threshold may not apply to MBBS.

💳 Study Cost

Review tuition, first semester payment, application, registration, lab/clinical charges, visa, EMGS, SST, insurance, bonds and living expenses.

🏥 Clinical Facilities

Ask about teaching hospitals, patient contact, clinical rotations, simulation labs and any placement restrictions for international students.

📋 Professional Recognition

Check programme accreditation plus professional board recognition in Malaysia and your intended practising country. A degree alone is not a licence.

✈️ Visa & Documents

Prepare passport, academic certificates and transcripts, English evidence, photos and other university/EMGS documentation required for your case.

Questions students ask

Healthcare education FAQ

Can international students apply for MBBS in Malaysia?

Selected programmes accept international applicants subject to academic equivalency, science grades, language skills, interview, seat availability and visa requirements.

Can I practise medicine in Bangladesh after a Malaysian MBBS?

Not automatically. Verify BMDC recognition, eligibility processes, internship and examination requirements before enrolling. Your home-country regulator has the final authority.

Can I do housemanship in Malaysia as an international graduate?

Do not assume eligibility. Housemanship and professional registration depend on current regulations, immigration status and placement availability. Plan your internship and registration pathway in advance.

How much does MBBS cost?

Total tuition depends heavily on university, nationality, intake and scholarships. Registration, visa, clinical fees and deposits may be additional. Request an itemised written quotation from HealthEd.

Is scholarship available for medicine, dentistry or nursing?

Availability depends on institution, intake, results, nationality and conditions. Check whether discounts cover the full course or only selected semesters.

Can I study physiotherapy, radiography or medical laboratory science?

Yes, these are healthcare-related alternatives to medicine. Entry requirements and professional registration differ by field and institution.

What if my visa is rejected after I have paid?

Refunds depend on the particular offer and written university policy. Ask which amounts are refundable, required supporting documents and processing timeframe before paying.

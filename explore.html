Questions answered

Healthcare admissions FAQs

What qualification do I need for MBBS in Malaysia?

Required science subjects, grades, accepted foundation/pre-university pathways, English tests and interviews depend on the university and your national qualification. We will check your exact profile.

Does a foundation guarantee admission into MBBS?

No. A foundation can provide an academic pathway, but progression into regulated professional degrees can require higher grades, interviews and available seats.

Can international graduates work as doctors in Malaysia?

Do not assume this. Housemanship, registration and work rights are governed by Malaysian regulation and immigration requirements. Confirm current rules before selecting a programme.

How can I calculate the total programme cost?

We recommend a semester-by-semester quote including tuition, first payment, registration, EMGS/visa, deposits, accommodation, insurance and any applicable tax. Fees can change by cohort.

What happens if my visa application is rejected?

Refund eligibility depends on the written policy, offer terms and reason for non-enrolment. Check non-refundable charges and required documents before making a payment.

Are all programmes available to international students?

No. Intake eligibility, professional regulations, placement availability and language requirements can restrict admissions. Every course needs individual confirmation.

Will I be redirected to a university website?

No. This portal presents programme information inside HealthEd and routes enquiries to Overseas Highway. Official documentation and university verification remain part of the admission process.
